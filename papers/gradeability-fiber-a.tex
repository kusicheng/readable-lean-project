Assume equality on \(\beta\) is decidable. Let \(s\) be a finite subset of \(\alpha\). Let \(t\) be a finite subset of \(\beta\). Let \(f\) be a function of type \(\alpha \to \beta\). \section{Results}
\begin{theorem}[{A fiber with weight above the bound for maps into the target}]\label{theorem:1}
Let \(s\) and \(t\) be finite subsets of \(\mathbb{N}\). Let \(f\) and \(w\) be functions of type \(\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\). Let \(b\) be a natural number. Assume \(\forall x\, :\, \mathbb{N},\; x \in s \implies f\left(x\right) \in t\). Assume \(\left|t\right| \cdot b < \sum_{x\in s}\left(w\left(x\right)\right)\). Then
\begin{center}
\(\displaystyle \exists y\, :\, \mathbb{N},\; y \in t \land b < \sum_{x\in \{x\in s \mid f(x) = y\}}(w(x)).\)
\end{center}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Prerequisite~P\ref{premise:4e61742e696e737449734f72646572656443616e63656c4164644d6f6e6f6964}, we obtain \(\text{translation preserves and reflects order on }\mathbb{N}\).
Use the hypothesis \(\forall x\, :\, \mathbb{N},\; x \in s \implies f\left(x\right) \in t\).
Use the hypothesis \(\left|t\right| \cdot b < \sum_{x\in s}\left(w\left(x\right)\right)\).
By Prerequisite~P\ref{premise:46696e7365742e6578697374735f6c745f73756d5f66696265725f6f665f6d6170735f746f5f6f665f6e736d756c5f6c745f73756d} with \(\alpha = \mathbb{N}\) and \(\beta = \mathbb{N}\) and \(M = \mathbb{N}\), we obtain \(\exists y\, :\, \mathbb{N},\; y \in t \land b < \sum_{x\in \{x\in s \mid f(x) = y\}}(w(x))\).

\end{proof}
